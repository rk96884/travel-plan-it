# Travel Plan It — project brief

Baseline: 8 September 2026. Internal working document based on the source conversation linked in ../README.md.

## Proposition

Travel Plan It is a proposed UK independent travel business creating highly personalised holidays and complex itineraries. Asia is the initial specialism, with tailored travel worldwide as the broader offer. The service brings together flights, accommodation, transfers, internal travel and distinctive experiences around an individual traveller's requirements.

The opportunity is to help travellers whose requirements are poorly served by straightforward package searches: multi-centre combinations, lesser-known places, unusual accommodation and specialist experiences. The customer supplies the brief; a consultant researches and shapes a coherent journey.

Working proposition: **Tailor-made travel, built around you.**

## People and operating model

The user reported that Rishi brings over 20 years of web development and digital technology experience plus marketing experience, and will lead brand, website, customer acquisition and technology. His business partner has approximately two years of recent experience at an ABTA travel agency and specialises in Asia and tailored long-haul travel. Another professional with over ten years of global travel experience is intended to join once the business is established; do not present that future role as a current employee.

The planned business is lean, primarily online/home-based and consultant-led. The founders are evaluating consortium or managed-service support for supplier access and operational infrastructure while retaining the Travel Plan It brand. No selected consortium, active licence or approved supplier account is established by the reviewed material.

## Working audience priorities

- Travellers planning a complex or multi-centre Asia trip who want knowledgeable help.
- Couples and families seeking a personalised long-haul holiday.
- Travellers seeking distinctive accommodation, destinations and experiences rather than a standard package.
- Prospective consortium and supplier teams checking whether the business is being established credibly.

These are website targeting proposals, not validated customer segments. Avoid positioning every trip as luxury or competing primarily on lowest price.

## Acquisition and service journey

Initial acquisition may combine purchased/third-party leads with the brand's own search, social and digital marketing. The user clarified that most of the partner's current leads come through Designer Travel. Whether that flow can transfer to the new agency is unknown.

Website journey: inspiration or destination page → Plan My Trip → structured brief → successful receipt → consultant follow-up → researched itinerary and quote → booking through the eventual approved operating workflow.

Revenue is expected to come from booking commission and/or agreed margin. Exact terms, fees and cash-flow timing remain pending. Earlier figures such as £200k–£250k first-year gross booking value are planning assumptions, not revenue, achieved sales or public claims.

## Objectives

1. Establish a credible visible brand for suppliers through the holding page.
2. Explain the Asia and tailor-made proposition clearly.
3. Generate useful, qualified enquiries for a consultant.
4. Build a maintainable content foundation for discovery and destination expansion.
5. Preserve a path to CRM and supplier integrations without building an OTA immediately.

Measure enquiry starts, completions, delivery failures, qualified leads and eventual conversion. Establish numeric targets after traffic and operational capacity are known. Analytics events must not contain personal enquiry data.
