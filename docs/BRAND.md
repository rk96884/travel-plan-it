# Travel Plan It — brand direction

## Name and idea

Use **Travel Plan It** in prose and metadata. A wordmark may use TRAVEL PLAN IT. Keep the spacing and avoid PlanIT. The user specifically favoured exploring the Plan It/Planet wordplay typographically. No final logo has been approved in the reviewed conversation.

Explore subtle spacing, weight or a restrained circular detail that suggests a journey or planet without making the name hard to read. A readable text wordmark is sufficient for the first build. Avoid a crowded mountains/water/sun/aircraft emblem. Do not explain the pun beneath the logo.

## Brand language

Primary working tagline: **Tailor-made travel, built around you.**

Secondary line: **Your planet. Your plan.**

Optional campaign lines from the discussion: “The world is yours. Plan it.” and “A world of possibilities. Planned around you.” Use selectively; do not crowd the page with multiple taglines.

Voice: warm, assured, knowledgeable and specific. British English. Describe what the traveller gains and how a consultant helps. Avoid superlatives, generic bucket-list language, invented testimonials and unsupported guarantees. Make the preparing-to-launch state explicit.

## Visual direction — proposed

A contemporary, premium long-haul/Asia feel: dark forest or teal, warm neutral surfaces, restrained coral accents and large destination photography. Suggested starting tokens, to validate for contrast during implementation:

| Role | Proposed colour |
| --- | --- |
| Primary forest | #173F3A |
| Deep text | #172C29 |
| Warm background | #F6F2E9 |
| Secondary teal | #326D68 |
| Coral accent | #C9664F |
| Light surface | #FFFFFF |

Do not assume every colour pairing is accessible. Use coral decoratively unless its contrast passes for the intended text size. Pair a characterful editorial heading face with a highly legible body sans serif; verify font licences and performance before selection.

Use generous spacing and strong typography. Photography should show a sense of place and the experience of travelling, with restrained overlays that keep text readable. Obtain licensed images and useful mobile crops. Do not imply an illustrated itinerary or hotel is an available product.

## Holding-page draft copy

**TRAVEL PLAN IT**  
Tailor-made travel, built around you.

### A new way to plan extraordinary travel.

Travel Plan It is an independent travel business preparing to launch, specialising in tailor-made holidays, complex itineraries and long-haul travel.

From multi-centre adventures and family escapes to carefully planned journeys across Asia and beyond, we combine experienced travel planning with modern technology to create trips built around the individual traveller.

**Our full website is coming soon.**

**Planning a trip?** Get in touch to register your interest.

**Tailor-made** — Trips designed around your requirements rather than off-the-shelf itineraries.

**Long-haul expertise** — Specialist knowledge of Asia, multi-centre and complex international travel.

**Human expertise. Smarter technology.** — Experienced travel consultants supported by modern digital tools.

**Your planet. Your plan.**

This is draft copy for implementation. Confirm public business details and the readiness to receive enquiries. Do not imply bookings are open or attach a response-time promise until operationally confirmed.

## Full-site copy direction

Lead the homepage with the same proposition and a Plan My Trip call to action. Follow with Asia expertise, how planning works, sample journey inspiration, why the team is building the service and a final enquiry invitation. Examples are inspiration, not live offers. About copy must distinguish current founders from future team members.
