# Enquiry retention policy

Decision recorded 9 September 2026: unsuccessful enquiries are retained for up to 12 months after closure. This is a business policy, not a statutory travel-enquiry deadline. Its proposed purpose is handling related follow-up questions and retaining the context of the enquiry. Review necessity and remove unneeded data earlier.

## Operational process required before launch

- Mark the closure date when a traveller declines or the enquiry is otherwise concluded. Do not leave inactive enquiries open indefinitely; review inactive enquiries monthly and close those no longer being pursued.
- Record a deletion date 12 months after closure. Review monthly and delete or irreversibly anonymise due records, including relevant inbox, sent, archive, deleted-item and exported copies.
- Handle deletion requests when received; do not insist on retaining data for the full year where it is no longer needed.
- Confirm the mailbox provider's retention, backup and deletion controls and Resend's logs/retention. Document how residual backup copies are put beyond ordinary use and expire.
- Keep only necessary records longer for a documented legal obligation or active complaint/dispute. Record why, who authorised the exception and its next review date. This must not become indefinite retention of every enquiry.
- Apply a separate justified schedule to records that become bookings before taking bookings.

No automatic mailbox deletion has been implemented. The present website delivers email without a CRM or persistent enquiry database. The business must implement this process in its mailbox and provider settings before making the policy live.
