# Travel Plan It — Social and Influencer Strategy

**Status:** Working strategy  
**Parent:** `00-MARKETING-STRATEGY.md`

## 1. Objective

Use social channels to demonstrate itinerary expertise, create demand and generate reusable creative learning without requiring a large internal content team.

## 2. Channel roles

- **Instagram:** primary visual brand/discovery channel; Reels, carousels, Stories and proof.
- **Facebook:** reuse suitable creative, retargeting and relevant audience/community reach.
- **TikTok:** short-form creative testing and discovery.
- **YouTube Shorts:** reuse strong vertical educational/inspirational creative with potential longer discovery life.

Do not create platform-specific publishing obligations until evidence justifies the workload.

## 3. Content pillars

1. Itinerary breakdowns.
2. Planning mistakes.
3. Destination combinations and routing.
4. Budget/value explanations using verified indicative figures.
5. Expert answers to common questions.
6. Destination inspiration with a planning lesson.
7. TPI process / how a trip is built.
8. Later: real customer journeys and testimonials with permission.

## 4. Publishing model

Prefer repeatable series over isolated posts.

Examples:

- **Would we build it this way?**
- **Two weeks, how would we plan it?**
- **Add it or skip it?**
- **The route matters**
- **What your budget needs to cover**
- **One change we'd make to this itinerary**

One core idea should generate multiple hooks and formats.

## 5. Community management

Comments and DMs can reveal:

- objections;
- destination questions;
- budget uncertainty;
- itinerary problems;
- future content topics;
- potential enquiries.

Create a simple process to move genuine planning enquiries into the approved customer enquiry/CRM flow rather than leaving commercial conversations buried in social DMs.

## 6. Influencer/creator strategy

Do not begin with expensive broad-reach travel influencers.

Test creators where there is:

- strong audience overlap;
- credible engagement;
- suitable UK/customer geography;
- content quality compatible with TPI;
- clear deliverables and usage rights;
- measurable referral/lead mechanism.

Potential models:

- creator produces UGC-style assets for TPI channels/ads;
- destination/itinerary collaboration;
- tracked referral/affiliate arrangement where permitted;
- paid post after audience-fit validation.

A creator's production ability may be more valuable than follower count.

## 7. Influencer due diligence

Record:

- audience geography/demographics where available;
- average/recent engagement;
- content relevance;
- listed/quoted price;
- deliverables;
- paid-media usage rights;
- exclusivity;
- disclosure requirements;
- tracking method;
- resulting enquiries/bookings.

## 8. AI and creator content

AI-generated creative and human creator content should complement each other.

AI is useful for rapid concept testing and variants. Human creator footage may provide authenticity, lived experience and distinctive production that synthetic creative cannot.

## 9. KPIs

Track:

- meaningful video retention/watch behaviour;
- saves/shares/comments with planning intent;
- profile/site actions;
- enquiries;
- qualified enquiries;
- creator/referral leads;
- bookings and retained margin;
- cost per usable creator asset;
- paid performance where usage rights permit.

Follower growth is secondary.

## 10. Launch posture

Pre-launch, build enough content to establish the brand and test formats. Avoid trying to fill every channel daily. A sustainable cadence with strong reuse is preferable to high-volume low-value publishing.
