# Travel Plan It — Paid Media Strategy

**Status:** Working strategy  
**Parent:** `00-MARKETING-STRATEGY.md`

## 1. Objective

Use paid media as a controlled learning and demand-capture mechanism, not as the assumption that makes the Year-1 plan work.

## 2. Principles

- Start small.
- Optimise beyond clicks and raw leads.
- Separate high-intent capture from discovery.
- Feed offline/CRM outcomes back into decision-making where technically practical.
- Scale only after lead quality and economics are understood.
- Stop tests that cannot plausibly reach sustainable CAC.

## 3. Google Search

Initial campaigns should focus on high-intent themes close to TPI's proposition.

Potential structures:

- tailor-made Asia;
- Thailand itinerary/planner;
- Japan itinerary/planner;
- multi-centre Asia;
- selected special-trip/honeymoon intent if validated.

Use negative keywords and search-term review aggressively to reduce irrelevant bargain/DIY traffic.

Landing pages should match the query rather than sending every ad to the homepage.

## 4. Meta

Meta should test:

- itinerary-led video;
- planning mistakes;
- budget/route hooks;
- destination inspiration tied to expertise;
- retargeting once sufficient audience volume exists.

Avoid judging video solely on views. The useful progression is:

**creative response → landing-page behaviour → enquiry → qualified enquiry → booking**

## 5. TikTok / other paid social

Do not add paid platforms simply for coverage. Organic creative can test audience/format fit first. Paid expansion follows evidence.

## 6. Creative testing

Test materially different hypotheses:

- expertise;
- time saving;
- complexity reduction;
- destination/route;
- budget framing;
- special-trip motivation.

Within a winning concept, then test hook, opening visual, duration, CTA and format.

## 7. Budget stages

### Stage 0 — tracking validation

Minimal/no media. Confirm analytics, conversion events, enquiry capture and attribution.

### Stage 1 — learning

Small budgets across a limited number of hypotheses. Seek enough signal to identify obvious failures/winners.

### Stage 2 — validation

Increase spend on promising combinations and evaluate qualified-lead and booking economics.

### Stage 3 — scale

Increase only while CAC, margin and operational capacity remain acceptable.

No fixed numerical media budget is set here until retained booking economics and available launch capital are agreed.

## 8. CAC

Do not compare CAC to TTV.

For each campaign calculate, as data allows:

- spend;
- enquiries;
- qualified enquiries;
- quotes/proposals;
- bookings;
- booked TTV;
- retained gross margin;
- CAC;
- contribution after acquisition.

## 9. Brand safety / accuracy

Travel advertising must not use invented or stale:

- prices;
- availability;
- flight schedules;
- visa/entry rules;
- hotel claims;
- protection/membership claims.

Indicative price-led creative needs clear context and current verification.

## 10. Stop/scale framework

**Scale:** repeatable qualified demand + acceptable economics + capacity.

**Iterate:** promising engagement/lead quality but weak conversion with an identifiable funnel issue.

**Stop:** persistent low-quality demand, no credible path to allowable CAC, misleading audience fit or operational burden disproportionate to value.
