# Travel Plan It — Marketing Launch Plan

**Status:** Working plan  
**Parent:** `00-MARKETING-STRATEGY.md`

## 1. Objective

Launch with enough marketing infrastructure to learn and convert, without delaying the business for unnecessary polish.

## 2. Gate 1 — strategy and membership preparation

Complete:

- master marketing strategy;
- audiences and positioning;
- acquisition model;
- TNG/TTA business-plan marketing summary;
- outstanding membership/commercial questions.

**Exit:** TPI can explain who it targets, why it is differentiated and how £200k–£250k Year-1 TTV could be generated.

## 3. Gate 2 — commercial/regulatory model

Confirm selected host/membership arrangement and document:

- permitted branding;
- consumer protection wording;
- booking/payment flow;
- supplier access;
- commission/margin handling;
- B2B permissions;
- required customer documentation.

**Exit:** marketing can accurately describe what TPI sells and under what structure.

## 4. Gate 3 — acquisition foundations

Build/confirm:

- production website structure;
- analytics/conversion tracking;
- enquiry journey;
- CRM pipeline;
- email acknowledgement/follow-up;
- core SEO pages;
- social profiles;
- campaign naming/UTM conventions;
- experiment register.

**Exit:** a lead can be acquired, attributed, qualified and followed through to booking.

## 5. Gate 4 — creative proof of concept

Produce initial Thailand, Japan and multi-country Asia creative.

Test ChatGPT/OpenAI, Creatify and Zeely as appropriate.

Record:

- cost;
- production time;
- usable assets;
- quality;
- organic response where published.

**Exit:** TPI has a repeatable initial creative workflow and launch asset bank.

## 6. Gate 5 — soft launch

Activate controlled:

- organic social/video;
- SEO indexing/content distribution;
- network/referral outreach;
- selected B2B discovery;
- small paid-search/social tests once tracking is verified.

Avoid large launch-day media spend.

## 7. Gate 6 — first 30 days

Review weekly:

- enquiries;
- source;
- qualification;
- response time;
- creative performance;
- search terms;
- technical failures;
- early costs.

Fix funnel issues before scaling traffic.

## 8. Days 31–90

Begin evaluating:

- audience quality;
- enquiry-to-quote progression;
- initial booking conversion;
- ABV;
- margin;
- CAC;
- content/search traction;
- B2B pilot potential.

Reallocate effort toward channels showing commercial promise.

## 9. Months 4–12

Scale proven acquisition carefully, deepen successful content clusters and improve CRM/referral processes.

Replace planning assumptions in `10-YEAR-1-ACQUISITION-FORECAST.md` with actual data.

## 10. Lean launch checklist

A launch is viable when:

- the proposition is clear;
- membership/commercial claims are confirmed;
- core pages work;
- enquiry capture works;
- analytics works;
- follow-up ownership is clear;
- initial content/creative exists;
- customer-service capacity exists.

A launch does **not** require every destination page, automation, social channel or campaign idea to be complete.
