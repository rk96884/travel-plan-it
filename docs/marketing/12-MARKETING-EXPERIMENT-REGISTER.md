## 5. EXP-001 — AI video production workflow

**Status:** READY

**Hypothesis:**  
A lean AI-assisted workflow can produce credible, branded short-form TPI creative fast enough and cheaply enough to support continuous testing without a dedicated video-production team.

**Brief set:**

- Thailand itinerary;
- Japan pacing;
- multi-country Asia complexity.

**Candidate routes:**

- ChatGPT/OpenAI;
- Creatify;
- Zeely AI.

**Controls:**

Use substantially equivalent concepts, target duration and output format. Record differences in tool capability rather than forcing identical production mechanics.

**Capture for each output:**

- tool/plan;
- date;
- direct cash cost;
- human minutes;
- generation attempts;
- usable outputs;
- editing required;
- authenticity/accuracy issues;
- final asset ID;
- organic results;
- paid results if subsequently promoted.

**Initial success criterion:**

Identify at least one workflow capable of producing repeatable publishable/near-publishable 9:16 creative with acceptable human review effort and cost.

Do not select a permanent paid platform from demo quality alone.

## 6. Evidence rules

- Preserve enough information to reproduce the test.
- Do not retrospectively change success criteria to make a test appear successful.
- Separate observation from interpretation.
- Record failed experiments; they are useful evidence.
- Avoid declaring winners from tiny samples.
- Commercial metrics override vanity metrics as data matures.

## 7. Review cadence

Review active experiments weekly during launch periods and the full register monthly.

Validated learning should be reflected back into the relevant strategy document rather than living only in this register.
