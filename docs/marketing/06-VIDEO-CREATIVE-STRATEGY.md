# Travel Plan It — AI Video and Creative Strategy

**Status:** Working strategy / experiment design  
**Parent:** `00-MARKETING-STRATEGY.md`

## 1. Objective

Create a lean, repeatable short-form video engine that demonstrates real Travel Plan It expertise while using AI to reduce production time and cost.

The strategy is tool-agnostic. ChatGPT/OpenAI, Creatify and Zeely are candidate production routes, not predetermined winners.

## 2. Creative principle

**Expertise creates the idea. AI accelerates the execution. Human review protects accuracy and trust.**

TPI should not publish generic destination montages with interchangeable AI copy simply because they are inexpensive to produce.

## 3. Production workflow

1. Select a real customer problem or itinerary question.
2. Define the expert answer/angle.
3. Create 3–5 materially different hooks.
4. Write a short script/storyboard.
5. Assemble verified source facts.
6. Produce variants using the selected AI/tool workflow.
7. Human-review travel facts, visuals, claims and brand tone.
8. Export platform-appropriate vertical creative.
9. Publish/test organically where appropriate.
10. Record results.
11. Promote/iterate winners.
12. Feed learning back into the experiment register.

## 4. Tool test

Test equivalent briefs through:

### ChatGPT/OpenAI

Evaluate for:

- concept generation;
- hooks/scripts/storyboards;
- visual asset generation;
- available video-generation capability;
- iterative creative direction;
- ability to support the wider content workflow.

### Creatify

Evaluate as a purpose-built ad/video production workflow, including its usefulness for rapid performance-ad variants.

### Zeely AI

Evaluate its video/ad creative workflow and any campaign-management value separately from pure production quality.

Pricing/features change frequently; current costs should be recorded in the experiment register at the time a paid test is approved rather than hard-coded as permanent assumptions here.

## 5. Comparison scorecard

For every tool/workflow capture:

| Dimension | Measurement |
| --- | --- |
| Cash cost | Subscription/credits + platform-specific fees |
| Production time | Human minutes from brief to approved export |
| First-pass usability | % outputs close to publishable |
| Visual authenticity | Human review |
| Brand consistency | Human review |
| Editability | Ease of correcting weak outputs |
| 9:16 suitability | Technical/creative review |
| Voice/avatar quality | Where applicable |
| Platform reuse | Number of useful destinations/formats |
| Organic response | Retention/engagement/CTA signals |
| Qualified demand | Enquiries and qualified enquiries |
| Commercial result | Bookings/CAC when sufficient data exists |

## 6. Prototype 1 — Thailand

### Concept

**"What can a well-planned two-week Thailand trip actually look like?"**

Do not make a fixed price claim until a live, verified example exists.

### Hook variants

- "The biggest Thailand mistake? Trying to fit everything into two weeks."
- "Bangkok, the north and the beach — here's how we'd make the route work."
- "Planning Thailand? The order of your stops matters more than you think."

### Story structure

0–3s: strong problem/hook.  
3–12s: show route/decision.  
12–25s: explain why the sequencing works.  
25–35s: one expert caveat/choice.  
Final: soft CTA — ask TPI to help design the trip.

### Test

Create comparable versions through available ChatGPT/OpenAI, Creatify and Zeely workflows.

## 7. Prototype 2 — Japan

### Concept

**"Your first Japan itinerary doesn't need to become a race."**

Hooks:

- "Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka is the easy part. It's what you add that can ruin the pacing."
- "If your Japan itinerary looks like this, you may be moving hotels too often."
- "Fourteen days in Japan: here's where we'd start."

Focus on itinerary pacing and expertise rather than generic cinematic Japan imagery.

## 8. Prototype 3 — Multi-country Asia

### Concept

**"Three countries doesn't always mean a better trip."**

Hooks:

- "The best multi-country Asia itinerary may involve removing a country."
- "Before you book three countries in two weeks, check the travel time between them."
- "A complex itinerary should feel like one trip, not three separate holidays."

This directly demonstrates TPI's complex-itinerary positioning.

## 9. Formats

Prioritise:

- 9:16 vertical;
- 15–45 second tests initially;
- captioned/sound-off comprehensibility;
- strong first 1–3 seconds;
- clear branding without overwhelming the creative;
- CTA appropriate to awareness level.

Reuse across Instagram Reels, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube Shorts where platform fit is acceptable.

## 10. Authenticity controls

Before publication confirm:

- destination shown matches narration;
- generated visuals do not depict misleading hotels/experiences as the booked product;
- no fake customer testimonial;
- no invented pricing/availability;
- no unconfirmed protection/membership claim;
- no fabricated team expertise;
- AI avatar/voice does not misleadingly impersonate a real TPI consultant.

Where synthetic media disclosure is required by a platform or applicable rule, follow it.

## 11. Organic-to-paid loop

Where practical:

**produce cheaply → publish organically → identify promising hook/creative → refine → run controlled paid test → evaluate qualified demand**

Organic performance is a signal, not proof of paid conversion.

## 12. Asset naming

Use consistent IDs, for example:

`VID-TH-001-HOOK-A-V1`

Where:

- VID = video;
- TH = Thailand;
- 001 = concept;
- HOOK-A = hook variant;
- V1 = production version.

Record tool, prompt/brief version, date and results in the experiment register.

## 13. Initial success definition

The first test is successful if TPI learns:

- which workflow produces usable branded video fastest;
- approximate cost per usable asset;
- which creative angles earn meaningful attention;
- whether the process can be repeated without heavy manual production.

Bookings are the ultimate metric, but the earliest pre-launch experiments primarily validate production capability and audience response.
