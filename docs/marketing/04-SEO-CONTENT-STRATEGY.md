# Travel Plan It — SEO and Content Strategy

**Status:** Working strategy  
**Parent:** `00-MARKETING-STRATEGY.md`

## 1. Objective

Use genuinely useful itinerary and destination expertise to create compounding organic discovery while supporting paid campaigns, social creative, email and sales conversations.

## 2. Principle

TPI should not build a generic travel blog. Content should sit close to a commercial planning problem that TPI can solve.

A useful content test is:

> Would somebody planning a complex trip save time or make a better decision after reading this?

## 3. Initial content clusters

### Thailand

Potential topics:

- 10/14/21-day itinerary structures;
- Bangkok + north + beach combinations;
- Phuket vs Koh Samui within a wider route;
- how many nights per stop;
- first-timer planning mistakes;
- indicative budget-led itineraries.

### Japan

Potential topics:

- first 10/14-day routes;
- Tokyo/Kyoto/Osaka sequencing;
- adding regional stops;
- family/couple itinerary variants;
- seasonal considerations;
- when an itinerary becomes too rushed.

### Multi-country Asia

Potential topics:

- combinations that route logically;
- when two countries are better than three;
- stopover opportunities;
- flight/routing implications;
- realistic trip length.

All time-sensitive entry, price, transport and operational facts require verification and appropriate dating.

## 4. Page architecture

The website should ultimately distinguish:

- destination hub pages;
- itinerary/route pages;
- planning guides;
- commercial enquiry pages;
- trust/about/process content.

Content should link naturally toward a relevant enquiry action.

## 5. Search intent

Prioritise:

1. **High commercial intent:** tailor-made / planner / itinerary help.
2. **Commercial investigation:** best route, how long, where to combine.
3. **Informational with downstream value:** planning questions that demonstrate expertise.
4. **Broad inspiration:** only where it can feed the content/video ecosystem efficiently.

## 6. Content flywheel

One researched itinerary can produce:

- core itinerary landing page;
- supporting article(s);
- FAQ;
- short-form video scripts;
- carousel/social content;
- email;
- paid creative;
- consultation talking points.

Record the canonical source material so facts do not drift across variants.

## 7. AI use

AI may assist:

- topic clustering;
- outlines;
- first-draft copy;
- FAQs;
- script adaptation;
- metadata;
- internal-link suggestions.

Human review is required for destination accuracy, brand voice and travel facts. Avoid mass publishing low-value AI pages.

## 8. On-page foundations

Each priority page should have:

- one clear search/user purpose;
- useful original expert input;
- descriptive title/H1;
- readable URL;
- logical internal links;
- strong enquiry CTA;
- suitable structured information where technically appropriate;
- analytics events for meaningful engagement/conversion.

## 9. Content prioritisation score

Score proposed content against:

- commercial relevance;
- expertise advantage;
- audience fit;
- search opportunity;
- reuse potential;
- production effort;
- seasonality/timeliness.

Do not use search volume as the only priority signal.

## 10. Initial launch content target

Prefer a smaller useful launch library over dozens of thin pages.

Initial target hypothesis:

- 3 strong destination hubs;
- 6–10 high-value itinerary/planning pages;
- core process/about/trust pages;
- supporting FAQs.

Exact scope should follow keyword research and website capacity.

## 11. KPIs

- non-brand organic sessions;
- search impressions/clicks;
- ranking coverage for relevant intent;
- assisted and direct enquiries;
- qualified enquiry rate by landing page;
- booked TTV/margin influenced by organic;
- content-to-video reuse efficiency.
