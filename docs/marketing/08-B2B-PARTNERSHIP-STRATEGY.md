# Travel Plan It — B2B Partnership Strategy

**Status:** Working hypothesis pending host/membership confirmation  
**Parent:** `00-MARKETING-STRATEGY.md`

## 1. Objective

Test whether Travel Plan It can build a second growth engine by supplying specialist long-haul/Asia itinerary capability to businesses or travel professionals that already own customer relationships.

## 2. Core hypothesis

Independent consultants and concierge/lifestyle businesses can encounter complex travel requests that require substantial research, itinerary design and pricing effort.

TPI may be able to provide specialist support while the partner retains the client relationship, subject to the legal, booking, payment, protection and commercial rules of the selected TNG/TTA/host arrangement.

## 3. Priority partner types

### Independent travel consultants

Potential need:

- complex Asia request;
- lack of destination expertise;
- insufficient research time;
- difficult multi-centre routing;
- overflow capacity.

### Concierge/lifestyle businesses

Potential need:

- specialist travel capability without maintaining a full travel operation;
- complex client requests;
- trusted fulfilment/referral partner.

### Complementary premium service businesses

Test selectively where customer overlap is credible.

## 4. Potential service models

These are hypotheses requiring host approval:

1. itinerary research/design only;
2. itinerary research + pricing;
3. trade fulfilment/booking;
4. referral arrangement;
5. white-label or co-branded support where permitted.

Do not promise a model before commercial and regulatory responsibility is clear.

## 5. Partner value proposition

Working proposition:

> **Specialist long-haul itinerary support when your client request needs more research, routing expertise or capacity than you have available.**

Benefits to test:

- retain client relationship;
- extend destination capability;
- reduce research burden;
- faster response;
- access specialist itinerary thinking;
- scalable overflow support.

## 6. Discovery questions

For travel professionals:

- How often do you receive complex long-haul/Asia requests?
- Which requests consume the most research time?
- What do you do when expertise/capacity is missing?
- Would you outsource research, pricing, booking or all three?
- What turnaround is expected?
- How would you expect commission/margin to work?
- What must remain invisible/visible to the end customer?
- What would prevent you using a trade partner?

## 7. Commercial model

For each possible partner arrangement capture:

- who contracts with the traveller;
- who takes payment;
- whose booking terms apply;
- who carries customer service responsibility;
- protection/licensing position;
- supplier access;
- gross commission/margin;
- TPI share;
- partner share;
- transaction/host costs;
- cancellation/amendment responsibility;
- payment timing.

## 8. Pipeline

Track:

**Prospect → contacted → discovery → qualified partner → commercial proposal → pilot → active → repeat**

Record estimated opportunity separately from consumer TTV until the economics are understood.

## 9. Initial outreach

Founder-led, personalised outreach is preferable to mass B2B email.

Start with a tightly defined list of potential partners and a discovery objective rather than a hard sales pitch.

## 10. Pilot design

A good pilot should answer:

- Can TPI deliver the work efficiently?
- Is turnaround acceptable?
- Is margin attractive after labour and host costs?
- Does the responsibility split work operationally?
- Does the partner return with another request?

## 11. TNG/TTA dependency

Before activating the service, obtain written clarity where necessary on:

- permitted B2B/trade activity;
- branding;
- supplier use;
- ATOL/package protection responsibilities;
- booking/payment flow;
- commission/margin handling;
- documentation;
- customer ownership/data handling.

## 12. KPIs

- qualified partner conversations;
- pilots;
- conversion to active partner;
- requests per active partner;
- turnaround time;
- gross/retained margin per request;
- repeat rate;
- partner-sourced TTV;
- operational time per booking.
