## Instagram / Facebook Reel — Version A

Thailand in two weeks? More stops don't automatically make a better trip.

A route such as Bangkok → Chiang Mai → Koh Samui can give you three very different sides of Thailand without turning every other day into another transfer.

The right itinerary depends on your dates, interests, budget and pace — which is exactly why we believe complex trips deserve proper planning.

Five stops in Thailand in two weeks? Sometimes the best itinerary starts by removing one.

Bangkok → Chiang Mai → beach. Fewer boxes to tick. More holiday to enjoy.
