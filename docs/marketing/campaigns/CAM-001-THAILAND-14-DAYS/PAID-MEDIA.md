# CAM-001 Paid Media Plan

**Status:** HOLD until booking proposition, tracking and membership claims are ready.

## Meta

### Audience hypothesis

UK adults showing relevant Thailand/long-haul travel intent, with broad/algorithmic targeting tested against more explicit interests once sufficient conversion signals exist.

Final targeting must be configured using current Meta options at launch.

### Creative tests

- Hook A — mistake
- Hook B — expertise
- Hook C — challenge

Keep the destination/body/CTA sufficiently consistent initially to learn about hook differences.

### Optimisation

Do not launch for video views as the commercial end goal. Once technically ready, optimise around the most meaningful available conversion event and evaluate qualified enquiries downstream.

## Paid launch gate

Do not spend until:

- landing page works;
- enquiry form works;
- analytics events verified;
- CRM/source capture verified;
- commercial/consumer-protection wording confirmed;
- someone owns lead response;
- test budget/CAC guardrail approved.
