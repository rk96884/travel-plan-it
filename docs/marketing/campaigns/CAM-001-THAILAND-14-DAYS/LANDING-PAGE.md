# CAM-001 Thailand Landing Page Brief

## Working URL

`/thailand/14-day-itinerary/`

Final information architecture should follow the production site's SEO conventions.

## Primary purpose

Turn campaign/search interest into a qualified Thailand planning enquiry.

## Hero

**H1:** Thailand in 14 days — build a trip that has time to breathe

**Support:** Bangkok, the north, the islands — there are dozens of ways to combine Thailand. We'll help design the route around your dates, interests, budget and pace.

**CTA:** Plan my Thailand trip

## Page structure

1. Hero + CTA.
2. Why 14 days requires choices.
3. Example three-part itinerary: Bangkok / Chiang Mai / beach.
4. "Why this route might change" — season, interests, flight routing, pace.
5. Alternative itinerary patterns.
6. How Travel Plan It works.
7. Enquiry form.
8. FAQs.
9. Confirmed protection/membership information only once approved.

## Lead fields

Keep initial form practical:

- name;
- email/phone as appropriate;
- approximate dates/flexibility;
- party;
- approximate budget;
- places/interests;
- what they want help with.

Marketing consent must be handled separately as required.

## Measurement

Track:

- campaign/source;
- landing-page visit;
- CTA click;
- form start;
- form submit;
- qualified status downstream;
- quote;
- booking;
- TTV/margin.

## Accuracy

The page should explain that example itineraries are illustrative. Do not hard-code prices, schedules or entry requirements without a maintenance/verification process.
