# Travel Plan It — Channel Strategy

**Status:** Working strategy  
**Parent:** `00-MARKETING-STRATEGY.md`

## 1. Objective

Build a diversified, measurable acquisition mix capable of supporting the Year-1 £200k–£250k TTV target without creating unnecessary fixed marketing cost.

## 2. Channel operating model

Channels have different jobs:

- **Demand capture:** high-intent search and referrals.
- **Demand creation:** short-form video, social and inspirational content.
- **Trust building:** useful itinerary content, expert explanations, reviews and email.
- **Nurture/recovery:** CRM, remarketing and abandoned/incomplete enquiry follow-up.
- **Partner acquisition:** direct B2B outreach and relationship development.

TPI should avoid expecting every channel to convert on the first interaction.

## 3. Priority channels

| Channel | Primary role | Launch priority | Cost posture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SEO/content | Capture + trust | High | Time-led / low media cost |
| Google Search | Capture | High | Controlled paid tests |
| Organic short-form video | Discovery + trust | High | Lean AI-assisted production |
| Meta paid social | Discovery + retargeting | Medium–High | Test then scale |
| TikTok organic | Discovery/testing | Medium | Low fixed cost |
| YouTube Shorts | Discovery/search longevity | Medium | Reuse vertical creative |
| Email/CRM | Nurture + reactivation | High | Low marginal cost |
| Referral | Trust + conversion | High | Build process early |
| B2B partnerships | New demand source | High test | Founder-led initially |
| Influencers/creators | Reach + social proof | Test | Performance/controlled basis |

## 4. Search

Search should focus initially on queries where expert planning can add value rather than competing broadly for generic holiday terms.

Examples of intent families:

- multi-centre Asia itineraries;
- Thailand/Japan itinerary planning;
- destination combinations;
- tailor-made long-haul;
- complex route questions;
- honeymoon/special-trip planning where relevant.

SEO and paid search should share keyword and conversion learning.

## 5. Social/video

Social is primarily a discovery and expertise-demonstration engine.

Content pillars:

1. itinerary breakdowns;
2. planning mistakes;
3. route/destination combinations;
4. realistic budget framing;
5. expert tips;
6. inspiration with a clear planning angle;
7. behind-the-itinerary expertise.

One vertical creative should be reusable, where appropriate, across Instagram Reels, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

## 6. Email/CRM

Capture permission at useful points in the journey and use CRM for:

- enquiry follow-up;
- quote/proposal follow-up;
- useful destination nurture;
- re-engagement;
- post-trip referral/repeat activity.

Avoid creating newsletters simply to satisfy a publishing calendar.

## 7. Partnerships

Founder-led outreach should test:

- independent travel consultants;
- concierge/lifestyle businesses;
- complementary premium service businesses;
- relevant referral networks.

Track partnership leads separately from consumer marketing.

## 8. Attribution

At minimum retain:

- first known source;
- latest/lead source;
- campaign/UTM;
- landing page;
- enquiry date;
- qualified status;
- booked value and margin.

Customers may cross multiple channels, so avoid assigning strategic value solely from last-click data.

## 9. Budget principle

No channel receives a large permanent budget at launch. Investment increases when evidence supports:

- qualified demand;
- acceptable CAC;
- sufficient retained margin;
- operational capacity to service additional enquiries.

## 10. Review cadence

Weekly during active tests: spend, leads, lead quality and technical issues.

Monthly: channel economics, creative performance, funnel movement and budget reallocation.

Quarterly: positioning, audience/channel mix and major strategic changes.
