## Commercial questions to resolve

1. Which exact membership and protection arrangement fits the proposed itineraries, and what approved wording can the website use?
2. What are all joining, recurring, booking, passenger, card, insurance and exit costs, including VAT treatment and renewal timing?
3. What commission or margin is retained, and when can profit be released under the actual payment workflow?
4. Are Aviate, Lime and Flightstore available through the consortium, with separate accounts or shared onboarding? What turnover, credit or deposit terms apply?
5. What permission and fare conditions apply to combining flight products with independently sourced ground arrangements?
6. Can Dynamic support the intended complex multi-centre itineraries? Arrange a practical demonstration.
7. What approval and protection process applies to independent specialist suppliers?
8. Who owns customer data, what can be exported, and what happens on exit?
9. Can independently generated/purchased leads be used, and can the partner's present lead source serve Travel Plan It?

## Technical discovery — separate from commercial terms

Prior ChatGPT research mentioned InfiniteAPI and InfiniteCache in the Aviate/Lime/Flightstore ecosystem. These are unvalidated integration leads for this handover. Obtain authoritative product documentation and written access terms before choosing an implementation.

For each integration record: account owner, product name, documentation version, sandbox availability, authentication, permitted uses, API fees, rate limits, cache freshness, search/quote/book/ticket capabilities, servicing/cancellation support, support contact and contractual restrictions. Confirm whether customer-facing publication is allowed. Portal access alone does not imply API access.

Phase 1 has no dependency on flight APIs. Keep enquiry delivery independent of the final flight supplier. Never copy a supplier's ATOL, ABTA or IATA identifiers into Travel Plan It's footer as its own credentials.
